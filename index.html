Game Description

Step into the world of kawaii fashion in Left or Right: Anime Dress Up! Your job? Choose between two adorable outfit options and build the perfect anime look. From cute school uniforms to glittery fairy costumes, your fashion sense decides it all. Show off your style and unlock new characters, themes, and accessories as you level up. Every decision matters — left or right?
